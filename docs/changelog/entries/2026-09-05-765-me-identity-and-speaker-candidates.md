### Resolve Me speaker name from profile and wire speaker candidate evidence

- **Issue:** `#765`
- **PR:** `pending`
- **Changed:** Pluto now correctly displays the user's configured name (e.g., `User (You)`) in meeting transcripts for all meetings, including historical meetings recorded before voice-enrollment metadata was captured. Speaker candidate embeddings produced by FluidAudio diarization are now persisted to `meeting_speaker_candidates` via the final transcription pipeline, enabling cross-meeting voice recognition to improve automatically.
- **Why:** Historical recordings had `capture.origin: 'unknown'` because no capture record was journaled, causing the `Me` speaker binding to silently fail. The frontend `extractSpeakerDisplayNames` also lacked a fallback path to the user's profile name. Separately, `clusterEvidence` from FluidAudio was never forwarded into `applyRemoteSpeakerClusters`, so speaker embeddings were discarded and cross-meeting recognition could not improve over time.
- **Replaced:** `getMeetingIdentityContext` required `capture.origin === 'local'` to bind `Me`; `extractSpeakerDisplayNames` had no profile or selfPersonId fallback; `runFinalTranscription` called `applyRemoteSpeakerClusters` without `clusterEvidence` or `provenance`.
- **Notes:** Three targeted surgical changes: (1) relax origin gate in `electron/commitmentIdentity.ts` to accept `'unknown'` while preserving `'capture'`-source evidence for `'local'`-only acoustic meetings, (2) add `profile.preferredName` → `selfPersonId` lookup fallback in `src/components/features/meetingTranscriptPresentation.ts`, (3) add `clusterEvidence` optional field to `FinalSpeakerEvidence` and forward it in `src/services/finalTranscription/runFinalTranscription.ts`.
