### Make Projects reflect durable work across conversations

- **Issue:** [#719](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/719)
- **PR:** Direct `master` delivery requested by the owner.
- **Changed:** Projects now synthesizes notes-grounded themes across conversations and presents current focus, recent change, open threads, and conversation history as the primary portfolio briefing.
- **Why:** One meeting previously produced an authoritative-sounding project immediately, leaving the real recurring work hidden among extracted fragments and every dossier stuck on insufficient health evidence.
- **Replaced:** Per-meeting transcript project promotion, health-first portfolio grouping, and sparse dossiers dominated by empty status cards.
- **Trust:** An automatic theme needs exact support from at least two structured-note sources. Existing one-meeting discoveries move to a separate review area, and user-confirmed project identities and names remain authoritative.
- **Recovery:** Suggestions can be confirmed or dismissed without deleting source evidence. Synthesis is idempotent for an unchanged notes corpus and an incomplete model result can be retried explicitly.
- **Performance:** One bounded portfolio synthesis replaces independent discovery attempts for every meeting and yields while recording or foreground intelligence is active.
- **Notes:** Project search includes both established themes and discussed work, while completion, rename, merge, milestone, commitment, and meeting-navigation controls remain available.
