### Use one editable meeting document

- **Issue:** [#641](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/641)
- **PR:** pending
- **Changed:** Pluto now presents legacy and current analysis as one continuously editable, outcomes-first meeting document with a preserved live scratchpad, contextual transcript evidence, visible local save state, persistent next-step completion, and regeneration recovery.
- **Why:** Separate analysis viewers, tabbed notes, dashboard cards, and raw topic inventories made the product feel generated rather than written and obscured the decisions and next steps users need when they return.
- **Replaced:** Version-specific meeting viewers, a competing Notes/Transcript tab product, noninteractive action checkboxes, and overwrite-only regeneration.
- **Notes:** Repeated and housekeeping topics are omitted, oversized analysis is capped at six coherent sections, and the full transcript remains available as a supporting record.
