### Notes as prep, owner lines, live notes editing and Ask Pluto beside meetings

- **Issue:** Notes, meetings, people and Ask Pluto journeys for PR #894.
- **PR:** [#894](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/pull/894)
- **Changed:** A note chosen for an upcoming meeting is linked to its prep instead of copied, so later edits show there. The recording rail offers a recently edited note about the meeting or its people, and live notes are edited like Notes, with checkboxes, bullets and headings. Ask Pluto answers can become a to-do or a "Next time with …" item, and Ask Pluto and search read notes added after a meeting. "Next time with …" items say when they came up in the latest meeting with that person. A checklist line like "Priya: send deck" in any note shows on Priya's "Next time with …" and in prep for meetings with her, labeled "On Priya's list". On a finished meeting, "Ask Pluto" (or ⌘J) opens the same right-hand panel Notes uses, beside the notes. Picking a slash command removes the typed "/…", even a partial one; Person and Project swap it for "@" or "#" so typing filters the list, and Task in meeting notes puts the caret inside "[task: ]".
- **Why:** Copies drifted from their notes, notes written after a meeting were invisible to questions and search, items assigned to people in group notes stayed inside the note, live notes showed raw `- [ ]` text, the floating Ask box covered the end of the notes, and a leftover "/" stayed in the line after choosing a command.
- **Replaced:** Copying a note's text into prep notes; prep now keeps note ids in `meeting_prep.note_ids_json` (migration `0021_prep_notes`). The floating composer on finished meetings; the recording dock keeps it and shares the request logic (`useMeetingAskPluto`).
- **Notes:** "Came up in" uses word matching on the transcript and skips notes edited after the meeting began. A first name counts only when no other known person shares it. Tasks still have no owner.
