### Turn meeting evidence into one send-ready follow-up

- **Issue:** `#506`
- **PR:** `pending`
- **Changed:** Follow-up Drafts now presents one editable Email, Internal, or Slack variant at a time, composes concise credential-free drafts from supported evidence, preserves format-specific edits in a versioned saved document, and keeps optional refinement aligned to the same facts.
- **Why:** The previous three-card surface repeated Pluto metadata and meeting analysis instead of helping users review and copy a message they would actually send.
- **Replaced:** Three simultaneous metadata-heavy editors, manual primary-position saving, action-or-decision-only availability, and model instructions for unrelated drafts.
- **Notes:** Pluto still does not send messages or require model credentials; weak evidence produces an honest compact explanation instead of filler.
