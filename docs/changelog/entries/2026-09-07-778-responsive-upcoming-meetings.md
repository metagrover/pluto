### Show more of today's calendar at a glance

- **Issue:** [#778](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/778)
- **PR:** [#779](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/pull/779)
- **Changed:** The dashboard's collapsed Upcoming meetings agenda now shows three rows in compact windows and five in the larger layout, keeps overflow behind an accessible `More` disclosure, and presents a clear `No meetings today` state.
- **Why:** Two visible rows hid too much of a busy day, while an unbounded list would crowd Pluto's daily briefing.
- **Replaced:** The fixed two-row collapsed limit and the less direct `No more meetings today` copy.
- **Notes:** Calendar fetching, ordering, recovery states, and Pluto's existing visual language are unchanged.
