### Align People with the meeting workspace

- **Issue:** [#644](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/644)
- **PR:** Pending.
- **Changed:** People now uses compact, single-action relationship rows, a quiet priority divider, and the same search, hover, focus, and disclosure language as All Meetings.
- **Why:** The previous directory-like grid made relationship context feel visually separate from the meeting workspace and obscured the most recent actionable signal.
- **Replaced:** Large multi-column person rows with a separate Open button and a prominent directory heading.
- **Notes:** The first viewport still prioritizes people with open commitments and recent conversation; inactive relationship records remain available through the existing disclosure.
