### Reframe Knowledge and People as memory briefings
- **Issue:** [#389](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/389)
- **PR:** Pending.
- **Changed:** Knowledge now leads with one operating picture—Current Read, attention, and risks—while deeper streams and source inspection move behind Browse memory. People now presents searchable relationship rows with recent conversation, context, frequency, and a direct path back into the latest meeting. Browser preview data now exercises the relationship layout instead of failing on unsupported knowledge-graph calls.
- **Why:** The homepage established a clearer model for Pluto: orient through synthesis, recency, and evidence before exposing the archive. Knowledge and People still behaved like stacked admin cards and a generic contact directory.
- **Replaced:** Equal-weight Knowledge containers and repeated People profile cards with low information density.
- **Notes:** Existing Knowledge trust states and synthesis controls remain intact. People derives its briefing from existing person-to-meeting associations; no new persistence model was introduced.
