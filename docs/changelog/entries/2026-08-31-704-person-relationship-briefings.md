### Turn People profiles into trusted relationship briefings

- **Issue:** [#704](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/704)
- **Changed:** Selecting a person now opens a concise dossier with verified open expectations, recent deliveries, source-backed relationship insights, and separate confirmed, scheduled, and mention-only meeting history.
- **Trust boundary:** Confirmed speaker identity and explicit user ownership are authoritative. Calendar names and meeting mentions stay visibly provisional, and uncited or weak insights are omitted.
- **Capacity:** Cross-meeting knowledge refreshes now wait for 15 quiet minutes, wall power, safe thermals, and an unused foreground lane. Interrupted work remains queued for a later safe window.
- **Notes:** Meeting notes and meeting-scoped extraction still finish first. The idle work updates existing knowledge documents and does not introduce a new dreaming pipeline.
