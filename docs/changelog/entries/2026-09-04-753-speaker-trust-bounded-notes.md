### Restore source-correct speakers and put a hard bound on notes

- **Issue:** [#753](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/753), extending the speaker-trust work tracked in [#725](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/725).
- **PR:** Pending.
- **Changed:** Pluto removes System-correlated echo from the microphone transcript, attributes every surviving mic segment to `You`, and diarizes only System audio into participant speakers. Notes capacity uses the compact provider payload and ordinary oversized meetings receive no more than three leaves. Compact leaves reserve space for a real editor draft, retry one truncated writer response concisely, and may split a single persistently truncated leaf once.
- **Why:** Microphone diarization fragmented the user's surviving speech into generic speakers even though the other mic signal was loudspeaker echo already represented by System. Verbose or empty-draft budgeting also sent segmented meetings into long hierarchies or admitted editor prompts that failed only after a real draft existed; a leaf-level Ollama truncation or invalid editor response then discarded the whole meeting's notes.
- **Boundaries:** Direct notes use two model calls. Partitioned production notes use at most six, with no model merge, recursive repartition, or unbounded repair, and an admitted notes run ends after twelve minutes. A retry may replace a leaf audit with deterministic validation; plans that cannot meet the fixed bounds fail with a stable reason.
- **Trust:** Echo removal requires recovered-channel timing, text, and acoustic support. Unknown note sources, unsafe writer claims, cancellation, transport failures, and final full-source hierarchy failures still stop publication. Leaf audit fallbacks pass deterministic guardrails and final conservative acceptance, and surface a warning instead of claiming a clean model audit.
- **Replaced:** Diarizing mic audio into anonymous local speakers, sizing notes from the verbose internal prompt, and sending ordinary product runs into the recursive writer/audit/merge hierarchy.
- **Preserved:** Raw recordings, source ASR, exact note citations, stale-run checks, transactional publication, and historical compare-and-save behavior are unchanged.
- **Notes:** The legacy hierarchy remains available to explicit legacy and benchmark callers; it is no longer a production fallback for compact meeting notes. Repaired local Ollama editor guardrail findings are advisory only for non-root reviews.
