### Restore source-correct speakers and put a hard bound on notes

- **Issue:** [#753](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/753), extending the speaker-trust work tracked in [#725](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/725).
- **PR:** Pending.
- **Changed:** Pluto removes System-correlated echo from the microphone transcript, attributes every surviving mic segment to `You`, and diarizes only System audio into participant speakers. Notes capacity uses the compact provider payload and ordinary oversized meetings receive no more than three leaves. Compact writers receive 2,048 output tokens, plan against at most 8,000 source characters per leaf, and may replace one overflowing leaf with two smaller source-aligned leaves. All writers finish before optional editor calls. Direct Ollama notes retain a deterministically accepted writer draft when the bounded editor returns an invalid shape or fails a source guardrail.
- **Why:** Microphone diarization fragmented the user's surviving speech into generic speakers even though the other mic signal was loudspeaker echo already represented by System. Verbose or empty-draft budgeting also sent segmented meetings into long hierarchies or admitted editor prompts that failed only after a real draft existed. Output truncation, optional reviews started too near the twelve-minute deadline, and conditional willingness mislabeled as a commitment could then discard otherwise valid completed writer leaves.
- **Boundaries:** Direct notes use two model calls. Partitioned production notes use at most six, with no model merge, recursive repartition, or unbounded repair, and an admitted notes run ends after twelve minutes. The final fifteen seconds are reserved for validation and publication; an Ollama editor starts only while at least five minutes remain and receives a child deadline at that reserve. Remaining leaf reviews use deterministic validation. Plans that cannot meet the fixed writer bounds still fail with a stable reason.
- **Trust:** Echo removal requires recovered-channel timing, text, and acoustic support. Unknown note sources, unsafe writer claims, cancellation, transport failures, and final full-source hierarchy failures still stop publication. Direct and leaf audit fallbacks pass deterministic guardrails and final conservative acceptance, surface a warning instead of claiming a clean model audit, and expose only a sanitized validation category for diagnosis. Conservative fallback may reclassify source-evidenced conditional willingness as a point only by using the cited source text and clearing owner and due date; strict editor validation still rejects it.
- **Replaced:** Diarizing mic audio into anonymous local speakers, sizing notes from the verbose internal prompt, and sending ordinary product runs into the recursive writer/audit/merge hierarchy.
- **Preserved:** Raw recordings, source ASR, exact note citations, stale-run checks, transactional publication, and historical compare-and-save behavior are unchanged.
- **Notes:** The legacy hierarchy remains available to explicit legacy and benchmark callers; it is no longer a production fallback for compact meeting notes. Foreground notes hold the knowledge-synthesis pause across the admitted run. A read-only replay of the affected 31-minute source completed three writers plus one editor in 495,839 ms, skipped the remaining optional reviews, and returned a publishable result 224,161 ms before the hard deadline without database writes or competing inference.
