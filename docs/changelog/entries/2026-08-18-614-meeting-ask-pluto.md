### Add meeting-scoped Ask Pluto chat

- **Issue:** `#614`
- **PR:** `#636`
- **Changed:** Recording Zen mode now includes a right-aligned Ask Pluto composer that submits questions against a frozen, bounded snapshot of the active transcript and carries bounded prior turns for follow-ups. Its first intent-aware assistance mode locally recognizes catch-up, fact, decision, and action Recall requests, then applies an evidence-first response contract through the fast local live-meeting model task. Unavailable or timed-out generation falls back to a clearly labeled, deduplicated transcript excerpt. Main-process validation bounds every IPC field, live requests require the active recording owner, superseded requests are cancelled, and model-generated claims remain marked for review until their cited spans receive semantic validation. The stable-width dock renders quiet user bubbles and unframed Pluto turns, follows new messages while the reader is near the bottom, minimizes on outside click or from its header, restores from a compact bar, and preserves that minimized choice across Back home navigation for the lifetime of the recording session. Trust, claim, and citation metadata remain internal, while completed-meeting Ask Pluto stays model-backed.
- **Why:** Users need an assistant that recognizes the immediate job behind a meeting question, not a generic transcript search box. A bounded local Recall route answers the requested fact or catch-up directly without another classification generation, while the explicit fallback and near-bottom follow behavior keep the experience responsive and honest without taking priority from transcription.
- **Replaced:** A global-only Ask Pluto entry point and mocked inspector chat that did not preserve a meeting scope or return a typed trust/citation packet.
- **Notes:** Recall is the first assistance mode. Pluto now also has an embedded SQLite foundation for source-linked, append-only meeting-context events and immutable rolling-state revisions. No extractor or Ask Pluto retrieval path consumes that store yet, so this foundation does not change answers by itself. Understand, Advise, Organize, Create, Act, and Clarify remain independent follow-up slices, alongside an explicit Deep action in Zen, durable completed-note chat, corrections, and broader project/person/global Ask Pluto scopes under #62 and #612. In Zen mode, the chat dock reserves its own bottom grid row so the newest transcript lines remain unobscured.
