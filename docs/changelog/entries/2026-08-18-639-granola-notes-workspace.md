## Granola-like meeting notes workspace

- Makes the live scratchpad the primary recording surface and keeps the transcript alongside it as supporting context.
- Opens completed meetings on a calm Notes document with a secondary Transcript tab, compact controls, editable points, inline follow-ups, and contextual source reveal.
- Adds Auto, 1:1, Team sync, Customer call, Interview, and Project kickoff note templates without adding another analysis model.
- Constrains generated notes to a few coherent sections, merging repeated threads and omitting topic counts, screen sharing, and other non-outcome housekeeping.
