### Make meeting finalization converge before downstream work

- **Issue:** [#718](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/718)
- **PR:** Direct `master` delivery requested by the owner.
- **Changed:** Startup now classifies evidence-free orphan v3 journals as empty before attempting checkpoint repair, and both retry validation and Parakeet finalization validate canonical v2 trust envelopes before persistence. A newly sealed meeting remains `processing`; if restart interrupts audio materialization, startup reopens only the matching provisional generation, preserves its draft and user metadata, reconstructs mic, System, and mixed artifacts, and wakes the existing final worker. Complete journals use sequential source concatenation plus one timeline alignment operation instead of delaying and mixing every chunk.
- **Why:** Repeated orphan recovery failures and rejected transient save payloads made healthy startup state indistinguishable from active recording loss and left no durable evidence of which meeting transition failed. A later complete 37-minute dual-source capture exposed the inverse gap: its provisional row was falsely marked finalized, recovery skipped its sealed journal after restart, and final transcription never became eligible because no audio paths were persisted.
- **Replaced:** Retrying impossible empty journals on every launch, skipping incomplete sealed work merely because its meeting row exists, treating provisional persistence as completed finalization, reconstructing complete timelines through a many-input delayed mix, relying only on the database to discover malformed producer payloads, and logging a save stack without content-free meeting/run context.
- **Trust boundary:** Recoverable journals and explicit `needs_attention` meetings remain fail-closed. Validated and generation-mismatched rows are never reopened, final ASR waits for all three required audio artifacts, unverified simultaneous mic text remains neutral, the database still rejects invalid trust state, and downstream notes or intelligence cannot begin until canonical transcript commit succeeds.
- **Notes:** Failure diagnostics contain identifiers, transition class, validation reason, and rollback outcome but never titles, transcript text, or generated notes. Issue #739 may measure sealed-to-canonical cost, but its notes-cost ledger does not own or bypass recovery behavior.
