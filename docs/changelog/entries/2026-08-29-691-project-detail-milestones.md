### Make project progress easier to read and shape

- **Issue:** [#691](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/691)
- **PR:** [#692](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/pull/692)
- **Changed:** Project detail pages use a lighter editorial hierarchy with distinct attention, health, milestone, and momentum regions. Users can create, edit, complete, delete, and restore persisted milestones inline. User-created milestones remain separate from read-only checkpoints derived from confirmed commitments or meeting evidence.
- **Why:** Useful project evidence was presented with nearly uniform visual weight, making re-entry feel like reading a text blob. Users also lacked a durable way to define their own checkpoints.
- **Replaced:** The undifferentiated milestone table, prose-only health section, and read-only milestone experience.
- **Notes:** Analytics summarize observed meetings and commitments without a composite progress score. Missing evidence remains unknown, provenance is visible in words as well as color, and existing qualification, project merge, commitment, and source-meeting behavior is preserved.
