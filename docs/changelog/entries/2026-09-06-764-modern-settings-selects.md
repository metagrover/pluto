### Modern settings selection controls
- **Issue:** [#764](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/764)
- **PR:** [#774](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/pull/774)
- **Changed:** Role or field, Industry, Your person, and silence duration now use a shared themed selection control based on the Identify speakers picker.
- **Why:** Native select and datalist menus felt disconnected from Pluto's calm, consistent settings design.
- **Replaced:** Browser-native selection presentation with a Pluto-styled floating menu while preserving each field's existing values and persistence.
- **Notes:** Role and Industry still accept custom text; Your person remains searchable; silence duration remains a fixed option list.
