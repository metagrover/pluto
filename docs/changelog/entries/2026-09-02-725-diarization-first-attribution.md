### Verify speakers acoustically before publishing a final transcript

- **Issue:** [#725](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/725)
- **PR:** [#729](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/pull/729), [#735](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/pull/735), [#736](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/pull/736)
- **Changed:** Pluto's finalizer independently decodes microphone and System recordings, removes mic speech acoustically and temporally explained by System playback even when ASR words diverge, keeps System-origin speech remote, preserves mic-only speech as `Me`, requires mic-exclusive support before asserting `Me` during simultaneous speech, and derives confidence from evidenced retained duration. Historical retry still preserves unaffected saved wording and boundaries. Meetings carrying a completed `recovered_channel_acoustic_v1` result remain eligible for the explicit **Improve speaker labels** action so the newer v2 pass can replace them; the database claim gate permits that one-way upgrade, and the trust schema accepts its content-free reconciliation counters. The speaker-label retry finishes as soon as the validated transcript is safely committed while notes regeneration continues under its own status. Automatic notes recovery no longer borrows the user-visible manual retry state and now advances its bounded automatic-attempt budget. Completed v2 results do not offer another upgrade.
- **Why:** A real saved meeting showed that exact-word reconciliation misses echo when Parakeet misrecognizes the two channels differently. The subsequent label retry changed only 9 of 698 labels but marked the whole meeting verified, disabling conservative presentation for 144 overlapping `Me` candidates.
- **Replaced:** Meeting-wide confidence derived from the absence of `Unknown`, globally applying unsafe mixed-audio diarization clusters, and requiring three identical ASR words before acoustic System evidence can suppress mic echo.
- **Review follow-up:** Attribution now counts overlapping evidence intervals once and uses indexed interval queries. Unresolved historical labels cannot become `Me` without fresh channel ownership. Native evidence skips diarization only for exact digital System silence; quiet nonzero audio still receives the model pass. A recent 31-minute recording's gate median improved from 1.41 ms to 0.56 ms with identical output. A generated 1012-second silence fixture reduced native evidence from 3447 ms to 54 ms. Both real replay label counts stayed unchanged; no end-to-end speedup or ground-truth speaker accuracy gain is claimed. Detailed limits and measurements are in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-04-attribution-review-results.md`.
- **Notes:** Global diarization correctly failed closed. A checksum-verified local Whisper comparison over both complete 44m51s source recordings and 69 isolated changed intervals confirmed the reported echo and found that the first candidate wrongly demoted mic-only speech. The revised source-authoritative implementation removes that echo, retains the following local question as `Me`, keeps the overlapping remote response as `Them`, attributes 94.7% of retained speech, and leaves 232 unsupported simultaneous words `Unknown`. The v1-to-v2 repair remains manual and meeting-scoped; no automatic historical corpus rewrite occurs.
