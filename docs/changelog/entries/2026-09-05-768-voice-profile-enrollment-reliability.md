### Make remembered voices durable and visible in People Pro

- **Issue:** [#768](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/768)
- **PR:** Pending.
- **Changed:** Speaker review now carries each stored candidate's exact transcription revision into matching, rejection, and enrollment; enrollment remains available when automatic suggestions are disabled; and newly created People are enrolled through the person ID returned by the confirmed meeting binding. The native evidence pipeline now retains speaker-masked embeddings backed by clean system speech, filters only deviant observations when a stable majority remains, and keeps the existing purity threshold intact. Explicitly enrolled active profiles participate in future-meeting suggestions without a hidden flag, while disabled profiles remain inert. People Pro plays the same system-channel reference audio used by Speaker Identification and distinguishes a profile-loading failure from a person who has no enrolled voice.
- **Why:** Checking “Remember this voice for future meetings” could save the meeting identity while silently rejecting or skipping the voice enrollment. Valid production embeddings were also discarded when their public diarization span crossed short clean-speech intervals, leaving recurring peers unidentified and their People Pro Voice Profile empty.
- **Replaced:** Whole-span containment for already speaker-masked embeddings, placeholder `gen-1` candidate revisions, inert enrolled profiles behind a calibration flag, microphone-channel profile playback, silent enrollment failures, and People Pro's ambiguous empty state after a profile read failure.
- **Notes:** Voice matching remains an explicit, reversible suggestion rather than a silent identity overwrite. Existing failed enrollment attempts require an explicit evidence replay before they can be repaired; Pluto does not infer biometric consent from an identity binding alone.
