### Make remembered voices durable and visible in People Pro

- **Issue:** [#768](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/768)
- **PR:** Pending.
- **Changed:** Speaker review now carries each stored candidate's exact transcription revision into matching, rejection, and enrollment; enrollment remains available when automatic suggestions are disabled; and newly created People are enrolled through the person ID returned by the confirmed meeting binding. When final transcription did not persist a candidate, Pluto analyzes the same bounded system-audio samples offered in Speaker Identification, requires one unambiguous candidate to pass the existing purity gates, and persists the candidate plus enrollment in one transaction. Confirming a peer now attempts that local enrollment automatically. Reading People Pro profiles or preparing future-meeting suggestions also reconciles prior user-confirmed peers from retained eligible meetings, one canonical person at a time. The reconciliation is idempotent, excludes the workspace owner, isolates per-source failures, and preserves explicit profile deletion as an opt-out. Active profiles participate in future-meeting suggestions without a hidden flag, while disabled profiles remain inert. People Pro plays the same system-channel reference audio and distinguishes a profile-loading failure from a person who has no eligible voice evidence.
- **Why:** Checking “Remember this voice for future meetings” could save the meeting identity while silently rejecting or skipping the voice enrollment. Valid production embeddings were also discarded when their public diarization span crossed short clean-speech intervals, leaving recurring peers unidentified and their People Pro Voice Profile empty.
- **Replaced:** Finalization-only enrollment candidates, whole-span containment for already speaker-masked embeddings, placeholder `gen-1` candidate revisions, inert enrolled profiles behind a calibration flag, per-speaker enrollment checkboxes, microphone-channel profile playback, silent enrollment failures, and People Pro's ambiguous empty state after a profile read failure.
- **Notes:** Voice matching remains a reversible suggestion rather than a silent identity overwrite. A user-confirmed peer identity now authorizes local profile creation only when retained meeting evidence passes the existing clean-audio and purity gates; raw embeddings remain local and never enter renderer IPC. Existing meetings do not require transcript regeneration, and explicit deletion prevents automatic recreation.
