### Recognize people and preserve commitment decisions across regenerated notes

- **Issue:** [#679](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/679)
- **PR:** [#680](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/pull/680)
- **Changed:** Commitment extraction resolves source-grounded owner identity and compares obligation meaning against pending and reviewed history before publishing suggestions. Durable aliases retain source descriptions and reuse canonical commitments without resetting user decisions. Regenerating meeting notes atomically retires the earlier meeting-derived commitment snapshot before publishing its replacement, while normal publication no longer waits for owner resolution or semantic comparison model calls. Revisioned background reconciliation reacts to identity/source corrections and restores unsupported aliases. Optional About you onboarding and Settings capture preferred/alternate names and work/study context; existing users receive a dismissible invitation.
- **Why:** Reworded suggestions bypassed description hashes and resurfaced after confirmation or dismissal.
- **Replaced:** Text-normalization-only identity at the production extraction boundary.
- **Notes:** Each proposal requires independent source and pairwise verification; names, shared nicknames, team membership and channel labels alone cannot identify a person. Unresolved ownership stays separate. Different projects, deliverables, deadlines, and recurring occurrences remain distinct. Failed comparisons defer publication. Failed or superseded regeneration leaves the existing commitment set untouched; manually created commitments remain active, and retired meeting-derived records retain review history for recovery. Reviewed records and original evidence are retained, restoration reverses alias-created source links, and maintenance rejects outdated review plans. Capture-time self identity is immutable; imported or legacy recordings do not inherit the current profile. Deleting sources removes derived identity evidence. Work/study context is saved for future terminology work, not used to rewrite transcripts in this release. Frozen Qwen identity evaluation did not establish semantic accuracy: two cases failed safely, three hit sustained capacity, and nine were not evaluated; no wrong owner was published in the completed cases, but no accuracy rate is claimed.
