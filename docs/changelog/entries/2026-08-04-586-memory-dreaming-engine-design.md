### Make cross-meeting dreaming proposal-first and reviewable
- **Issue:** [#586](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/586)
- **PR:** [#587](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/pull/587)
- **Changed:** Implemented bounded, notes-first background preparation for People and Projects. Idle work runs through the shared preemptible inference coordinator, uses fixed local `gemma4:12b`, validates exact evidence and source revisions, and stores proposals without changing canonical data. Dossiers expose a calm prepared-updates review where each accepted or rejected change is applied through existing canonical structures with provenance, corrections, and stale-write protection.
- **Why:** Point-in-time extraction leaves useful knowledge fragmented, but giving a model direct graph authority would turn unsupported inference into durable memory. Proposal-only generation preserves local background utility while keeping every semantic change inspectable and reversible.
- **Replaced:** Direct model-authored project/person mutations, repeated full-corpus idle scans, manual force triggers, permissive validation, and the earlier idea of selecting or benchmarking Qwen for this pipeline.
- **Notes:** Gemma is the fixed model for evidence-heavy dreaming; Phi remains reserved for approved lightweight interactive paths. Raw transcripts are excluded, generated proposals are capped and revision-bound, foreground work preempts them, and automatic acceptance is outside this release.
