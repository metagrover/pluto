### Make meeting notes the primary workspace

- **Issue:** [#639](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/639)
- **PR:** [#640](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/pull/640)
- **Changed:** The live scratchpad is now the primary recording surface, completed meetings open on Notes, contextual source reveal supports generated points, and note templates reuse the configured analysis model.
- **Why:** Meeting re-entry should begin with the user's notes and a concise synthesis instead of a transcript or a dense analysis dashboard.
- **Replaced:** Transcript-first recording and completed-meeting layouts with equally weighted Notes and Transcript surfaces.
- **Notes:** Auto, 1:1, Team sync, Customer call, Interview, and Project kickoff templates remain available without adding a second analysis model.
