### Simplify the dashboard into a calm daily briefing

- **Issue:** [#655](https://github.com/metagrover/pluto/issues/655)
- **PR:** Not created; committed directly to master at the user's request.
- **Changed:** The first viewport now centers Top of mind, at most three confirmed commitments, and zero or one evidence-backed Recent Win. Users can add a confirmed commitment inline and celebrate a supported win with reduced-motion-aware feedback.
- **Why:** Pluto should do the sorting before the user reaches the dashboard instead of presenting another meeting archive or undifferentiated task feed.
- **Replaced:** The document-style Current Read hero and competing project, meeting, and knowledge panels in the first viewport.
- **Notes:** Unconfirmed follow-ups remain in a separate disclosure and cannot occupy Top of mind. Unsupported wins render an honest empty state.
