# Pluto product demo

[Watch the demo on the website](https://metagrover.github.io/get-pluto/#in-action) ·
[Open the video](media/pluto-demo.mp4) ·
[English captions](media/pluto-demo.en.vtt)

The approved demo is a 45-second, 2560 × 1440 recording of Pluto’s actual
interface using fictional Paper Lantern meetings. It shows meeting notes,
familiar voices, transcripts, project history, completing a commitment, and
Chat with Pluto with source references.

## Narration

So, this is Pluto. You can just have your meeting, and it takes the notes for you.

If you've spoken to someone before, Pluto can recognize their voice automatically.
You can check the names here.

And if you want to see exactly what someone said, just open the transcript.

Meetings about the same project stay together, so you don't have to piece
everything together yourself.

These are the things you need to do. Just tick them off when you're done.

Or you can ask a question, like, "What am I waiting on?"

It gives you an answer, and shows you the meetings it came from.

## Production notes

Approved October 13, 2026. The source is a native Retina window recording with
real app interactions and fictional demo data. Cursor movement and click
indicators were added in postproduction to match those interactions.

The final edit uses a muted dark blue surround, app-window margins, a steady
left navigation panel, and restrained content zooms. The voiceover uses
ElevenLabs Roger with an original soft music bed. The final framing edits
preserve the approved audio without re-encoding it. English caption timing
matches that narration.

Use fictional data for any replacement footage. Keep the 45-second pacing,
navigation context, readable transcript transition, and approved narration
unless a new edit is explicitly requested.
