# Pluto Brand Guidelines

## Core Philosophy
- **Intelligent & Proactive**: A sophisticated assistant that stays ahead of your needs.
- **Warm & Sophisticated**: Moving away from sterile "tech" vibes towards a premium, "study-like" aesthetic.
- **Second Brain**: Designed to feel like a curated extension of your own mind.

## Visual Identity

### Logo
- **Concept**: A stylized 'P' integrated with orbital paths and nodes, representing interconnected knowledge and planetary discovery.
- **Symbol**: The 'P' is formed by a continuous line, suggesting flow and process.
- **Tagline**: "Your Second Brain for Work" (Inter Regular)

### Typography
- **Primary Typeface**: **Inter**
- **Headings**: Inter Bold / Medium
- **Body**: Inter Regular
- **Character**: Highly legible, multi-purpose, modern sans-serif.

## Color Palette

| Name | Hex | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Midnight Blue** | `#1A2340` | Primary Brand Color, Headings, Primary Buttons, Active States |
| **Champagne Gold** | `#D4B483` | Accents, Icons, Highlights, Borders for Softness |
| **Warm Cream** | `#F8F5F0` | Main Background, Subtle Surfaces, Input Backgrounds |
| **Pure White** | `#FFFFFF` | Core Surfaces, Cards (with subtle shadows) |

## UI Elements

### Surface & Depth
- **Backgrounds**: Use **Warm Cream** (`#F8F5F0`) for the main workspace background.
- **Cards**: Use **White** (`#FFFFFF`) for cards and elevated components.
- **Shadows**: Soft, expansive shadows to create depth without harsh edges.
- **Corner Radius**: `1.25rem` (20px) for standard cards; `0.75rem` (12px) for buttons/inputs.

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Midnight Blue background with White text.
- **Secondary**: Transparent or White background with Midnight Blue border/text.
- **Hover States**: Subtle opacity shifts or slight darkening/lightening of the base color.

### Inputs
- **Search Bar**: Warm Cream background with a subtle border and the Midnight Blue text.
- **Iconography**: Use consistent line weights (1px or 1.5px) matching the Midnight Blue or Champagne Gold colors.

## Tone of Voice
- **Professional but Approachable**: Expertly helpful without being robotic.
- **Calm & Focused**: Minimizing distraction, maximizing clarity.
- **Empowering**: Positioning the user as the pilot, with Pluto as the sophisticated navigation system.
